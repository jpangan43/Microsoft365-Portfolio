# Microsoft Teams Configuration

## Proposed teams
- Management
- Finance
- HR
- Sales
- Operations
- Marketing
- IT

## Procedure
1. Confirm each team's business purpose and owner.
2. Create a pilot team and channels.
3. Assign at least one accountable owner and a backup owner.
4. Use private/shared channels only where the access model requires them.
5. Review guest access and external communication settings.
6. Test membership, meetings, file access, and channel behavior.
7. Document actual results.

## Example channels
**Operations:** General, Announcements, Projects, Procedures, Resources.

Use synthetic content only in the lab.
