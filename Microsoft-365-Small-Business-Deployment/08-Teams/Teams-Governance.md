# Teams Governance

Document the following decisions before scaling:
- Who can create teams?
- Who approves external guests?
- Who owns each team?
- How are inactive teams reviewed or archived?
- What naming convention is used?
- What information belongs in a team versus a SharePoint site?
- Who reviews membership changes?

## Suggested naming convention
`TEAM-<Department>-<Purpose>`

This is an example, not a mandatory Microsoft naming pattern.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Primary owner assigned
- [ ] Backup owner assigned
- [ ] Purpose and department documented
- [ ] Membership reviewed
- [ ] Guest access justified
- [ ] Lifecycle/review date recorded
