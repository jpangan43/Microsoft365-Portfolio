# User Offboarding Runbook

Offboarding should follow the client's HR/legal requirements and approved timing.

1. Confirm the authorized termination/transfer request and effective time.
2. Block sign-in at the approved time.
3. Revoke sessions/tokens using supported procedures where appropriate.
4. Remove group memberships and access grants.
5. Preserve/transfer mailbox and OneDrive data according to retention and legal requirements.
6. Review shared mailbox, Teams, SharePoint, application, and admin-role access.
7. Recover company devices and revoke sessions on managed devices where applicable.
8. Remove or reassign licenses only after data/retention requirements are checked.
9. Document actions and notify the authorized owner.

Do not delete accounts or remove licenses reflexively before checking retention, legal hold, mailbox, and data-preservation requirements.
