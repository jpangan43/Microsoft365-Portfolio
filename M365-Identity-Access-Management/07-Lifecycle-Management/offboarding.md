# Employee Offboarding Runbook

## Before action

Validate the request, effective date/time, approval, data-retention requirements, mailbox/OneDrive ownership, legal hold requirements, and any privileged access held by the employee.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the departure with the authorized manager or HR contact.
2. Inventory accounts, roles, groups, sessions, applications, devices, and owned resources.
3. At the approved time, block sign-in.
4. Revoke sessions and refresh tokens using supported controls; recognize that existing sessions/tokens may not terminate instantly.
5. Remove privileged roles and access no longer justified.
6. Remove assigned group memberships where appropriate; allow dynamic groups to recalculate from attributes and rules.
7. Arrange authorized mailbox delegation or conversion and transfer business-resource ownership as required.
8. Preserve data according to retention, legal, and business requirements.
9. Remove or reassign licenses only after assessing mailbox, OneDrive, retention, and recovery implications.
10. Remove access to connected third-party services as part of the wider offboarding process.
11. Verify sign-in is blocked and access is addressed.
12. Record approvals, actions, exceptions, and completion.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Sign-in blocked at approved time
- [ ] Sessions revoked where supported
- [ ] Privileged roles reviewed and removed if no longer needed
- [ ] Groups and resource permissions reviewed
- [ ] Mailbox and OneDrive/data-retention requirements addressed
- [ ] Licenses handled after data review
- [ ] Connected apps and other credentials reviewed
- [ ] Completion evidence and approvals recorded

**Do not delete the account or remove its license as the first step without evaluating data retention, mailbox conversion, legal holds, and recovery needs.**
