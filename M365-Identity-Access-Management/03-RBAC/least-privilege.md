# Least-Privilege Controls

- Do not use Global Administrator for routine email or productivity work.
- Protect privileged accounts with strong authentication and monitoring.
- Keep emergency-access arrangements separately documented and tested.
- Where licensed, evaluate Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management (PIM) for eligible just-in-time elevation and approval workflows.
- Review role assignments after job changes and departures.
- Never grant privileged roles to an account just to bypass a lab configuration problem.
- Record exceptions, risk acceptance, owner, and expiry date.
