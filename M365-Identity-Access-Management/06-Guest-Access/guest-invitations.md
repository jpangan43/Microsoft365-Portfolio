# Guest Invitations and External Collaboration

## Configuration procedure

1. Review **Identity → External Identities → External collaboration settings**.
2. Decide who may invite guests and configure permissions according to policy.
3. Review domain restrictions and cross-tenant access settings where applicable.
4. Invite a designated external test identity only where authorized.
5. Have the guest accept the invitation and complete required sign-in controls.
6. Assign access only to the approved group, application, SharePoint site, or Teams workspace.
7. Test both permitted access and denied access to unrelated resources.
8. Record the internal sponsor, business purpose, owner, review date, and expiry/termination process.

## Guest access rules

- Require a business justification and internal sponsor.
- Grant only the access needed for the collaboration.
- Review access regularly and remove it when no longer needed.
- Avoid adding guests to broad internal groups without explicit approval.

Reference: [Microsoft Entra External ID](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/external-id/).
