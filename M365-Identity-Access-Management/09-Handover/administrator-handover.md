# Administrator Handover Checklist

## Configuration summary

- [ ] User and group naming conventions documented
- [ ] User and group inventory reviewed
- [ ] Administrative role assignments and justifications recorded
- [ ] MFA and authentication method policies documented
- [ ] SSPR configuration and recovery process documented
- [ ] Guest invitation and access-review process documented
- [ ] Onboarding and offboarding runbooks reviewed
- [ ] Licensing dependencies and limitations documented
- [ ] Known risks, exceptions, and unresolved items listed
- [ ] Review schedule and responsible owners agreed
- [ ] Sanitized evidence attached or referenced
- [ ] Change history and approval references recorded

## Do not include

Passwords, MFA recovery codes, private keys, access tokens, secrets, personal data, or other sensitive material in GitHub or ordinary handover documents.
