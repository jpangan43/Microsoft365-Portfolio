# Requirements and Scope

## Business scenario

Contoso Design & Consulting is a fictional 30-person organization. This project defines identity lifecycle and access controls for a Microsoft 365 environment.

## In-scope

- User inventory and naming standards
- Assigned and dynamic security group design
- Administrative role planning and least privilege
- Authentication methods and MFA planning
- SSPR configuration and testing
- Guest invitation governance and access reviews
- Onboarding and offboarding procedures
- Acceptance testing and sanitized evidence

## Out of scope unless explicitly added

- Production tenant changes without approval
- Endpoint management deployment
- Full SIEM/SOC implementation
- Automated HR integration or identity provisioning
- Legal determination of retention obligations

## Requirements checklist

- [ ] Confirm tenant and licensing prerequisites.
- [ ] Identify business owner and technical owner.
- [ ] Approve naming conventions and department mapping.
- [ ] Define who may approve access and privileged role assignments.
- [ ] Define MFA and account recovery requirements.
- [ ] Define guest access approval and review frequency.
- [ ] Define data retention and offboarding requirements.
- [ ] Agree on evidence storage and change logging.
